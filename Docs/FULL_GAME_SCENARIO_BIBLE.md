## Campaign promise and emotional arc

The player begins in a home that feels safe because its people, routines and landscapes respond to them. Bilbo's leaving and Gandalf's warning gradually turn familiar spaces into places that must be left behind. The road then expands the party's sense of responsibility: Frodo carries the Ring; Sam keeps the journey human; the wider ensemble shoulders dangers Frodo cannot see. The end returns the player to familiar ground changed by loss and care.

Playable options deepen relationships, place knowledge and route literacy. They never substitute a success/failure ending for the film's causal chain. Consequence appears in dialogue, discovered lore, readiness, travel comfort and save state.

## Chapter treatments

### F1-01 — Prologue & Ring History
**Playable flow:** Begin with Bilbo's past and the Ring's history as a concise interactive prologue; land in the Shire before the birthday. Give the player a quiet Frodo/Sam household-and-garden loop, meet local residents, see Gandalf arrive, then let party preparations lead into the celebration. The player can help with small social and field tasks, explore the special Party Field entrance and open-air kitchen, and learn the village's routes before the crowd fills them.

**Turning point:** Bilbo's disappearance interrupts the celebration. Return to Frodo's point of view for the private farewell and the Ring's transfer. A short aftermath lets Frodo absorb the change before time advances.

**Safe window:** Firework errands, conversations, garden tasks, food delivery, and local-lore discoveries. Each rewards familiarity or relationship context; none delays the fixed departure indefinitely or teaches future Ring knowledge prematurely.

**Locked result / handoff:** Bilbo leaves; Frodo possesses the Ring. Bag End becomes the next chapter's investigation space.

### F1-02 — Shadow of the Past
**Playable flow:** In Bag End, Frodo examines the Ring and asks original, contextual questions. Gandalf establishes its danger and the link to Sauron. Sam is invited into the secret at the correct point. Packing, choosing what to carry, and making a quiet departure are playable rather than a menu-only summary.

**Parallel scenario:** After identifying the danger, switch to Gandalf's investigation route: seek counsel at Isengard, discover Saruman's betrayal, survive imprisonment on Orthanc, and escape to rejoin the main chronology at Rivendell. Use the parallel-lane save barrier; do not let Frodo or NPCs know Gandalf's later findings before the reunion.

**Safe window:** Packing, a final walk through Bag End, and private conversations with Sam or a small number of Shire residents. These change remembered dialogue and optional records, not the Ring's destination.

**Locked result / handoff:** Frodo leaves the Shire with Sam. Gandalf's Isengard route is tracked separately and returns at the planned chronology barrier.

### F1-03 — Road to Bree
**Playable flow:** Travel from Hobbiton toward Buckland. Merry and Pippin join through a character moment rather than a random party spawn. Read the road, hedge gaps, roots and ferry landing; use stealth cues to teach how the Black Rider hunts. The first hiding encounter is patient and player-led, with breathing, soil and hoofbeats carrying the threat.

**Safe window:** Conversations, footpath discoveries, a modest supply task and optional road lore before the first Rider. Do not add combat against a Ringwraith or let optional play reveal the Ring to the Shire population.

**Set piece:** A second pressure rise makes the ferry the clear destination. The player helps ready and cross the ferry; the far-bank look-back ends the Shire movement arc.

**Locked result / handoff:** The companions escape toward Bree. Save all four Hobbit states and the Ring knowledge flags at the ferry barrier.

### F1-04 — Bree & Weathertop
**Playable flow:** Enter Bree at night, navigate a crowded inn, handle attention after the Ring incident, and decide whether to trust Strider through dialogue evidence and companion response. Travel east with camp and route choices that change comfort and conversation.

**Turning point:** The decoy-room plan is followed by the Black Riders' attack; the party survives, but Frodo is wounded at Weathertop. Keep the wound as a persistent play-state and emotional change, not a fail screen.

**Locked result / handoff:** Arwen's Ford rescue remains fixed; the party races for Rivendell.

### F1-05 — Rivendell & Fellowship
**Playable flow:** Recover, reconnect with Bilbo, explore a layered refuge, and attend the Council through controlled viewpoints and clear knowledge states. Allow equipment, relationship and lore scenes between Council beats.

**Turning point:** The Council chooses the Ring's destruction and the Fellowship forms. Player presentation may affect conversation recall, not membership or mission.

**Locked result / handoff:** Nine depart together.

### F1-06 — Caradhras & Moria
**Playable flow:** Build companionship on the road, hide from Crebain, attempt Caradhras with readable environmental survival, and reach the West Gate. In Moria, navigation and the discovery of Balin's fate lead into the troll and Balrog escalation.

**Pacing:** Use the long dark traversal to alternate observation, puzzle, combat and quiet companion beats; do not make every passage a fight.

**Locked result / handoff:** Gandalf falls at the Bridge; the Fellowship escapes. Give grief a quiet transition before Lórien.

### F1-07 — Lórien & Anduin
**Playable flow:** Let the group recover among the Elves. Galadriel's encounter and the Mirror use sparse, player-readable images rather than choice-dependent prophecy. Gifts and relationship conversations become preparation for river travel.

**Locked result / handoff:** The boats carry the Fellowship toward Amon Hen; pressure between companions grows without revealing future events early.

### F1-08 — Breaking of the Fellowship
**Playable flow:** Travel the Anduin, reveal the Argonath, land at Amon Hen, and create a quiet space for Boromir and Frodo. After Boromir's fall to Ring pressure, the Uruk attack becomes a multi-character defense and pursuit. Preserve Boromir's dignity and let Merry/Pippin's capture and Frodo's choice happen in the fixed order.

**Locked result / handoff:** Boromir dies, Merry and Pippin are captured, Frodo chooses Mordor, and Sam follows. Open the three campaign lanes at a synchronized save barrier.

### F2-01 — Three Hunters / Captives
Play the hunters' evidence-reading pursuit alongside Merry and Pippin's captivity and escape. Separate the lanes only at clear forest thresholds. The hunters meet Rohan; the captives enter Fangorn. The routes converge around the forest/Rohan chronology without giving either group future knowledge.

### F2-02 — Fangorn & Gandalf Returned
Merry and Pippin meet Treebeard and learn the cost of the forest's damage. Let exploration and patient discussion give the Entmoot context. In the parallel lane, Gandalf returns transformed and reconnects with the hunters. The Ents' decision and Gandalf's return are fixed story results.

### F2-03 — Edoras
Approach Meduseld through the town's inhabited spaces, witness Théoden's restoration, and give the court a social scene about duty and fear. Optional conversations affect relationship memory, not the decision to evacuate. The people leave toward Helm's Deep.

### F2-04 — Road & Helm's Deep Preparation
Move with the refugees. Optional rescue, provisioning, route learning and defensive preparation give the player ownership before battle. The army's arrival starts the siege at a fixed story boundary; preparation affects presentation and support state only.

### F2-05 — Battle of Helm's Deep
Defend the wall, respond to the breach, retreat through a legible keep route, protect civilian lanes, and regroup companions. The dawn counterattack is the release. Avoid an endless arena or variable outcome that erases Rohan's survival.

### F2-06 — Frodo, Sam & Gollum
Capture Gollum, establish restraint/trust rules in original dialogue, cross Emyn Muil and the Dead Marshes, and let Ring pressure rise through sound and perception. The Black Gate closes the direct route and redirects the trio toward Ithilien.

### F2-07 — Ithilien & Faramir
Rangers capture the Hobbits; the player observes occupied Ithilien and Ranger routines before Faramir tests Frodo's truth. Faramir refuses to take the Ring. Osgiliath exposes the danger of the Ring to the war, then the trio escapes and resumes the Mordor route.

### F2-08 — Ents & Isengard
Discovery and dialogue feed the Entmoot, then traversal moves with the Ents toward Isengard. The assault and flooding are environmental set pieces with safe escape lanes, not an extra combat campaign. Record Isengard's changed world state for the next part.

### F3-01 — Isengard Aftermath / Palantír
Explore the flooded aftermath and reconnect the allies. Pippin touches the Palantír and sees Sauron's attention; Gandalf takes him to Minas Tirith while the remaining party continues its Rohan route. Commit both lane positions and knowledge states.

### F3-02 — Minas Tirith & Muster
Enter the White City through its terraces, meet Denethor's strained court, and participate in the beacon call. Parallel Rohan muster scenes give Merry and Éowyn clear agency. The call for aid proceeds even if optional duties are skipped.

### F3-03 — Paths of the Dead
At Dunharrow, Aragorn accepts the burden of the road. Build the Paths from sound, light and traversal puzzles rather than repeated ghost combat. The oath is answered and the southern route opens.

### F3-04 — Siege & Pelennor
Move from the city siege to the field battle as Rohan arrives. Keep civilian danger, battlefield routes and companion roles readable. Théoden's death remains fixed; Éowyn and Merry defeat the Witch-king together. The victory carries a visible cost.

### F3-05 — Cirith Ungol / Mordor
Climb toward Minas Morgul, cross Shelob's lair through perception and stealth, then switch to Sam's tower rescue. Return control to Frodo for the disguise march through Mordor. Rest stops offer tension relief but cannot change the route or Ring-bearers' survival.

### F3-06 — Black Gate & Mount Doom
Play the host's diversion as a parallel objective while Frodo and Sam make the final ascent. At the Ring crisis, do not offer a moral choice that overwrites the established causal chain: Frodo cannot cast the Ring away; Gollum's intervention completes the outcome. Crosscut the Ring's destruction, Sauron's fall and the rescue.

### F3-07 — Return & Farewell
Crown Aragorn, return to a peaceful Shire, and let the player revisit familiar homes and relationships. Close companion threads in short social scenes, then give Frodo's departure for the Grey Havens room to breathe before control returns to Sam's family life.

**Explicit source disposition:** Tolkien's Scouring of the Shire is not silently discarded. It is held for a compatibility decision because it conflicts with the supplied film continuity's peaceful Shire homecoming. It is not inserted into the film-core route without an explicit project decision.
