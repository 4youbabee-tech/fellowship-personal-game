# PART I — THE FELLOWSHIP OF THE RING

## Product vision
Part I covers the full narrative arc of *The Fellowship of the Ring* from the Shire to the breaking of the Fellowship.

The player primarily experiences the journey as **Frodo**, with companions behaving as persistent AI characters. Story interludes that Frodo does not witness (for example Gandalf/Saruman and Isengard) are initially handled as cinematics so the game remains focused and hardware-friendly.

The film's recognizable route and events are the story spine. Exploration expands around them without changing the major sequence.

---

## Chapter 00 — Prologue: The Ring
**Film basis:** Prologue / One Ring to Rule Them All

**Format:** cinematic first; optional playable historical vignette later.

**Purpose:** establish the Ring, Sauron, Isildur, Gollum, Bilbo and the historical stakes before the quiet Shire opening.

**No open world load:** pre-rendered or lightweight in-engine sequence to protect memory.

---

## Chapter 01 — The Shire
**Film basis:** The Shire; Very Old Friends; A Long-Expected Party; Farewell Dear Bilbo; Keep It Secret, Keep It Safe; The Shadow of the Past. Extended additions may include Concerning Hobbits, Green Dragon material, and Passing of the Elves.

**Playable region:** Hobbiton / Bag End / party field / Green Dragon / connecting lanes and fields.

**Primary characters:** Frodo, Gandalf, Bilbo, Sam, Merry, Pippin; Rosie and local Hobbits as world-life NPCs.

**Gameplay:**
- explore Hobbiton,
- meet Gandalf on the lane,
- move through the village activity shown around his arrival,
- visit Bag End,
- participate in the party,
- witness Bilbo's disappearance and departure,
- return to Bag End,
- learn the Ring is dangerous,
- prepare to leave with Sam.

**Design priority:** The Shire must feel safe, populated and loved before the tone changes.

---

## Chapter 02 — Keep Off the Road
**Film basis:** A Short Cut to Mushrooms; Bucklebury Ferry. Extended optional: Passing of the Elves.

**Playable region:** farmland, cornfield/vegetable field, wooded road, exposed hillside, forest chase, Brandywine ferry.

**Primary characters:** Frodo, Sam, Merry, Pippin, Black Rider.

**Gameplay:**
- travel with Sam,
- meet Merry and Pippin through the Farmer Maggot field incident,
- first Ringwraith stealth encounter,
- hide and control breathing/movement,
- flee through woodland,
- timed group escape to Bucklebury Ferry.

**Threat model:** Ringwraith is not a normal enemy; the correct response is avoidance and escape.

---

## Chapter 03 — Bree
**Film basis:** At the Sign of the Prancing Pony; The Nazgûl. Extended optional: Midgewater Marshes after departure.

**Playable region:** rainy Bree gate, narrow streets, Prancing Pony interior, guest rooms, road east.

**Primary characters:** Frodo, Sam, Merry, Pippin, Barliman Butterbur, Aragorn/Strider, Bree locals, Nazgûl.

**Gameplay:**
- enter Bree at night,
- navigate crowded streets that feel oversized from Hobbit scale,
- locate the Prancing Pony,
- investigate Gandalf's absence,
- social stealth in the common room,
- accidental Ring incident as scripted story event,
- meet Strider,
- survive the Nazgûl raid through deception rather than direct combat,
- depart with Strider.

---

## Chapter 04 — Weathertop and the Flight to the Ford
**Film basis:** A Knife in the Dark; Flight to the Ford.

**Playable region:** wilderness, Weathertop hollow and summit, forest paths, ford approach.

**Primary characters:** Frodo, Sam, Merry, Pippin, Aragorn, Arwen, Ringwraiths.

**Gameplay:**
- wilderness travel,
- camp preparation,
- Weathertop assault,
- Frodo receives Morgul wound as a story-critical failure state that continues the plot,
- wounded movement / altered perception,
- search for healing aid,
- Arwen arrival,
- horse chase to the Ford as a cinematic-heavy playable set piece.

---

## Chapter 05 — Rivendell
**Film basis:** Rivendell; Many Meetings; The Fate of the Ring; The Sword That Was Broken; The Evenstar; Council of Elrond; Bilbo's Gifts; Departure of the Fellowship. Extended-only memorial material is optional.

**Playable region:** recovery room, gardens, terraces, waterfalls, council courtyard, departure path.

**Primary characters:** Frodo, Sam, Merry, Pippin, Gandalf, Bilbo, Elrond, Aragorn, Arwen, Boromir, Legolas, Gimli.

**Gameplay:**
- recovery and reunion,
- free exploration of a limited Rivendell hub,
- character conversations,
- Council of Elrond as interactive cinematic,
- Fellowship formation,
- receive travel equipment,
- leave Rivendell.

**Tone:** sanctuary, beauty, memory, and the burden becoming larger than the Shire.

---

## Chapter 06 — The Ring Goes South / Caradhras
**Film basis:** The Ring Goes South; The Pass of Caradhras.

**Playable region:** rough country south of Rivendell, exposed mountain approaches, snow pass.

**Primary characters:** all nine Fellowship members; Saruman appears in intercut cinematic.

**Gameplay:**
- companion travel,
- tutorial for Fellowship formations and companion reactions,
- avoid/observe Crebain,
- snow traversal,
- exposure/stamina pressure,
- avalanche sequence,
- decision to abandon the pass.

---

## Chapter 07 — The Gates of Moria
**Film basis:** Moria.

**Playable region:** dark lake shore, West-gate, cliff wall, entry chamber.

**Gameplay:**
- inspect the hidden gate,
- environmental puzzle around the inscription,
- Watcher in the Water set piece,
- retreat into Moria as the outside route is sealed.

**Tone shift:** from dangerous travel to claustrophobic survival.

---

## Chapter 08 — A Journey in the Dark
**Film basis:** A Journey in the Dark; Balin's Tomb.

**Playable region:** immense halls, narrow passages, dwarf cemetery, three-way tunnel junction, Chamber of Mazarbul.

**Gameplay:**
- torch/staff-light guided traversal,
- maintain group cohesion,
- hints of Gollum following,
- environmental storytelling,
- Balin's Tomb discovery,
- escalating goblin attack,
- Cave Troll boss encounter,
- escape the chamber.

---

## Chapter 09 — The Bridge of Khazad-dûm
**Film basis:** The Bridge of Khazad-dûm.

**Playable region:** collapsing stairs, great halls, bridge and exit.

**Gameplay:**
- continuous escape sequence,
- collapsing stair traversal,
- Balrog reveal,
- Gandalf holds the bridge in cinematic/gameplay blend,
- Fellowship forced to flee,
- emotional aftermath outside Moria.

**Important:** Gandalf/Balrog dialogue should not be reproduced at length in game data; scene timing and emotional function are the reference.

---

## Chapter 10 — Lothlórien
**Film basis:** Lothlórien; Caras Galadhon; Mirror of Galadriel; Farewell to Lórien. Extended content adds useful character and gift material.

**Playable region:** luminous mallorn forest, elevated Elven city, Galadriel's mirror area, river departure.

**Primary characters:** Fellowship survivors, Haldir, Galadriel, Celeborn.

**Gameplay:**
- tense first contact with Elves,
- sanctuary after Moria,
- grief and companion conversations,
- Mirror vision interactive cinematic,
- receive equipment/gifts,
- depart by boats.

---

## Chapter 11 — The Great River
**Film basis:** The Great River; approach to Parth Galen.

**Playable region:** Anduin river corridor, camps, Argonath reveal, Parth Galen landing.

**Gameplay:**
- controlled river travel,
- companion dialogue,
- visual landmark sequence at the Argonath,
- growing distrust and Ring pressure,
- shore exploration before the break.

---

## Chapter 12 — The Breaking of the Fellowship
**Film basis:** Parth Galen; The Breaking of the Fellowship; The Departure of Boromir; The Road Goes Ever On.

**Playable region:** Parth Galen forest, Amon Hen ruins, riverside, final eastern shore view.

**Gameplay:**
- Boromir confrontation handled as story-critical encounter,
- Frodo escapes through ruins/forest,
- Uruk-hai assault erupts around the group,
- battle heard/seen while Frodo chooses to leave,
- Sam pursues Frodo and joins him,
- final cinematic cross-cuts Boromir's fall and Aragorn/Legolas/Gimli choosing pursuit.

**Part I ending:** Frodo and Sam look toward the road ahead; save state unlocks Part II project scope later.

# Region loading strategy
Each major chapter loads as a separate map or streamed group:
1. Shire
2. Shire outskirts + Ferry
3. Bree
4. Weathertop / Ford
5. Rivendell
6. Caradhras
7–9. Moria sublevels
10. Lothlórien
11. Anduin
12. Amon Hen

This is essential for the 2 GB VRAM target.

# Current implementation priority
Do **not** build all maps at once. Production order:
1. complete Chapter 01 greybox and systems,
2. make Chapter 01 visually convincing and performant,
3. build Chapter 02 using the same companion/stealth systems,
4. continue chapter by chapter.

The entire Part I is now planned, but the active build remains Chapter 01 until it is stable.
